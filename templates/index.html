About Shekhawati Ultra

Some races take you to the finish line.
Shekhawati Ultra takes you through a story.

Created by Ranbakure, Shekhawati Ultra is a road ultramarathon that celebrates the soul of a place through the language of running.

The roads of Shekhawati have carried merchants, artists, travellers, and generations of communities that shaped one of India's richest cultural landscapes. They have witnessed centuries of craftsmanship, resilience, and tradition. On race day, they will welcome runners from around the world to become part of that continuing story.

Every turn reveals something worth remembering — painted havelis, timeless architecture, vibrant local life, and the quiet beauty of Rajasthan's countryside. This is not a course designed simply to measure distance; it is a journey designed to create connection.

Whether you choose Origins (30K), Heritage (50K), or Legacy (100K), every kilometre invites you to experience Shekhawati with curiosity, respect, and purpose.

At Ranbakure, we don't begin with a route. We begin with a place. We listen to its stories, learn from its people, and only then create the race.

Because every race is our tribute to the place.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

📋 How do I register?

Registration is through Konfhub — click any Register or SELECT CHALLENGE button on this page to go directly to the registration portal.

⏰ What time does the race start?

All distances start at 6:00 AM IST on 27 September 2026 from MJF Sports Ground, Nawalgarh.

🎒 What should I carry?

Your race bib (provided at check-in), comfortable running gear, hydration pack (recommended for 50K & 100K), and a charged phone. Detailed kit list will be emailed pre-race.

🏨 Is accommodation available?

Yes — Nawalgarh has a range of stays from heritage havelis to budget hotels. We'll share recommended options with registered runners. Early booking advised.

🔄 Can I change my distance category?

Distance changes are possible up to 2 weeks before race day, subject to availability. Contact us via the registration portal.

📸 Will there be race photos?

Absolutely. Professional race photography is included — you'll receive digital photos post-race to remember your Shekhawati journey.
