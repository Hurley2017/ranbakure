About Shekhawati Ultra

Some races take you to the finish line.
Shekhawati Ultra takes you through a story.

Created by Ranbakure, Shekhawati Ultra is a road ultramarathon that celebrates the soul of a place through the language of running.

The roads of Shekhawati have carried merchants, artists, travellers, and generations of communities that shaped one of India's richest cultural landscapes. They have witnessed centuries of craftsmanship, resilience, and tradition. On race day, they will welcome runners from around the world to become part of that continuing story.

Every turn reveals something worth remembering — painted havelis, timeless architecture, vibrant local life, and the quiet beauty of Rajasthan's countryside. This is not a course designed simply to measure distance; it is a journey designed to create connection.

Whether you choose Origins (30K), Heritage (53K), or Legacy (100K), every kilometre invites you to experience Shekhawati with curiosity, respect, and purpose.

At Ranbakure, we don't begin with a route. We begin with a place. We listen to its stories, learn from its people, and only then create the race.

Because every race is our tribute to the place.
